A shared vocabulary keeps the domain model stable. Inventing synonyms for a
canonical term, such as "rules file" for configuration, makes rules, specs,
and agent reviews hard to follow. Short sentences in active voice are easier
to translate and read.

When a concept already has a term in TERMINOLOGY.md, use that exact term.
When you need a new term, add it to TERMINOLOGY.md as part of the same
change.
